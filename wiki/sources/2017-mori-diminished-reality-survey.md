## Summary
Diminished reality (DR) visually removes, hides, or sees through real objects in real time, the opposite direction to AR, which adds virtual objects. The survey classifies about 20 years of DR work by function, by processing procedure, and by device, and is written partly for students and beginners.

## Key claims
- DR conceals, eliminates, or sees through real objects in real time; like AR/MR, its inconsistencies are geometric, photometric, and temporal. (p. 1) `peer-reviewed`
- Four basic functions: diminish (degrade part of the view), see-through (show the hidden background), replace (cover a real object with a virtual one), inpaint (generate a plausible background). (p. 2)
- Most existing DR systems are video see-through. (p. 3)
- A typical procedure: background observation, scene tracking, detection of the region of interest, hidden view generation, composition. (pp. 3–4, Table 1)
- Hidden views can be generated by homography (planar approximation), 3D reconstruction, image-based rendering, or inpainting. (p. 7)
- In composition, alpha blending is cheap and sufficient for seams; Poisson blending is expensive but gives reasonable results. (p. 8)
- Evaluating DR needs ground truth (the same scene with and without the object), which is hard to obtain. (p. 10)
- DR on optical see-through displays is "virtually an unexplored area". (p. 10)
- The text says new publications were added up to 2017 in one place and up to 2016 in another. (pp. 2, 11)

## 中文說明
- Diminished reality（DR）是「讓真實物體在視覺上消失、被遮住或變透明」，方向跟 AR「加東西」相反。
- 四種功能：diminish（弱化）、see-through（看穿）、replace（用虛擬物件替換真實物件）、inpaint（補出背景）；其中 replace 就是本論文「改造外觀」在 DR 領域的名稱。
- 典型流程是：觀察背景、追蹤場景、偵測目標區域、生成被擋住的畫面、合成，跟本論文的 pipeline 很像。
- 多數 DR 系統是影像透視（VST）；光學透視（OST）上的 DR 幾乎沒人做。
- 不一致分成幾何、光度、時間三類，可以直接當 RQ2 的分析框架。
