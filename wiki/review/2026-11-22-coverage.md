## Where the set is thin or skewed

1. **Secondary sources, not primary papers.** Three of the four are surveys or a textbook. They frame related work well but cannot back specific methods or numbers; those need the primary paper.
2. **Generating virtual things, not restyling real things.** Ning covers generative AI that creates virtual objects and scenes in XR; CTGAN textures virtual 3D models. None of the four changes how a real object looks in a camera view, which is the core of this thesis. Until that literature is in, the wiki cannot say whether the gap is real or already filled.
3. **Display mismatch.** Itoh is about optical see-through headsets; MVP-1 runs in a phone browser, which is video see-through.

   > [!inference] To verify in Itoh's sections on color and occlusion
   > Optical see-through displays add light to the real scene, so they likely cannot darken a real surface. A Multiply-style blend that works on a phone may not carry over to see-through glasses. If confirmed, this is a design constraint for the later headset version.

4. **Time.** Itoh predates the diffusion era, CTGAN is GAN-based, and Ning's search stops at 2025 Q1. Nothing covers 2025–2026 real-time diffusion or video generation.
5. **Evidence strength of Ning.** It is a preprint; by its own account it searched Google Scholar and arXiv in addition to IEEE, ACM and ScienceDirect, and it calls its sample small. Its counts are useful for the gap statement (MR appears in 1 of 26 studies; natural-language input in 20; diffusion models in 14), but they stay `single-source` until a second review agrees.
6. **No platform or market sources.** The seed table in [[overview]] is still empty.
